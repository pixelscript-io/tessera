- [The signals runtime](/how-it-works/signals): `state`, `derived` and `effect`.
- [The renderer adapter](/how-it-works/renderer): how a template becomes elements.
- [CSS layers and scoping](/how-it-works/css): where styles go and why they cannot collide.
- [Auto-import resolution](/how-it-works/auto-imports): how a tag finds its component with no runtime registry.
- [The route manifest](/how-it-works/routes): how a folder of pages becomes routes.
- [Why contracts and enforcement](/how-it-works/why): the reasoning.
