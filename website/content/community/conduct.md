# Code of conduct

Be kind and be specific. This applies to everyone who takes part in the project, in any place it happens.

- Treat people with respect. Criticize code, designs and ideas, not the people who wrote them.
- Assume good faith, and say what you mean plainly. Ask when you are unsure what someone meant.
- Do not harass, threaten, demean or discriminate. That includes jokes that single people out for who they are.
- Accessibility is part of respect: describe images in what you post, and do not make people do extra work to take part.
- Disagreement is fine and expected. Decisions are made as described in [Governance](/community/governance); once made, give them a fair try.

Someone who breaks these rules may be asked to stop, and may be removed from the project's spaces. Report a problem to the project's author directly.
